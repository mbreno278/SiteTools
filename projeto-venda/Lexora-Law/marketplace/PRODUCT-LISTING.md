# Marketplace Listing Draft — Lexora Law v1.2.0

## Product Title
Lexora Law — Premium Law Firm & Attorney Website Template

## Short description
A refined, responsive HTML website template for law firms, attorneys, and legal consultants. Includes practice areas, attorney profiles, insights, consultation pages, light/dark themes, clean code, and complete documentation.

## Detailed description
Lexora Law is a multi-page static HTML template designed for legal practices that need a confident, editorial online presence. It pairs refined typography and a restrained color palette with responsive layouts and practical frontend interactions.

The package includes a homepage, firm overview, practice-area directory, attorney directory and profiles, legal insights archive and article pages, contact and consultation forms, FAQ, editable legal example pages, a style guide, and a 404 page. Built with HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript, it requires no framework, database, or build step.

CSS is fully organized with clear section comments and design tokens, making color and brand changes straightforward. JavaScript is clean, dependency-free, and well documented.

The included forms are demonstration interfaces only. They do not send or store submissions. All firm identities, attorney profiles, contact details, and legal content are fictional placeholders and must be replaced and reviewed before publication.
